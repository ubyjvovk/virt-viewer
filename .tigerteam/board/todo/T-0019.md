## Goal
The workflow's "Upload app bundle" step can never run: nothing invokes
`make-bundle.sh`, so `hashFiles('build/*.app/Contents/Info.plist')` is
always empty and the step is permanently skipped (`dylibbundler` is
installed for that never-taken path). Found independently by two stress
reviews (T-0013 N1, T-0012 N4). PM decision: build the bundle in CI rather
than dropping the step — it exercises the packaging path on every push.

## Context
`.github/workflows/macos.yml`. The build step runs
`bash build-aux/macos/check.sh`; `build-aux/macos/make-bundle.sh` expects
the meson build in `build/` (the default) and produces
`build/Remote Viewer.app`.

## Required change
Add one step between "Build and test" and "Upload meson logs":

    - name: Build app bundle
      run: bash build-aux/macos/make-bundle.sh

Keep the existing upload steps and the brew dependency list unchanged.
